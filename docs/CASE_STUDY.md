# Case Study: Building GARRISYNC

## The problem
Leaders of competitive mobile-game alliances run something close to a small organization: recruiting, onboarding, scheduling recurring battles across time zones, tracking attendance, and answering member requests. The tools they have — chat channels, spreadsheets, and manual reminders — don't give them one authoritative place to run operations, and the coordination work falls on a few volunteer officers.

## The product
GARRISYNC positions itself as an **alliance operating system**: the place where alliances *run*, with Discord acting as a delivery channel rather than the system of record. It pairs a Discord bot (where members already are) with a web Command Center for leadership.

## Approach
1. **Start with a real pilot.** Features were built and exercised with a pilot alliance before being offered more widely.
2. **Evidence-based claims.** A canonical feature truth sheet classifies every capability as live, in progress, planned, or blocked, and governs what may be said in demos, website copy, and outreach. Marketing claims that could not be verified were flagged for founder decision rather than published.
3. **Staged releases.** Changes move through candidate builds, acceptance review against a written contract, deployment with a documented rollback, and post-deploy verification.
4. **Operate it like a business.** Pricing research, a go-to-market plan, customer onboarding, backups and recovery rehearsals, cost accounting for AI features, and ongoing customer-driven improvement.

## Capabilities built (high level)
Join onboarding and managed invites · leadership briefs · routed leadership requests · availability heatmap planning · event/RSVP/reminders · recurring-battle scheduling · attendance and reporting · recruitment pipeline · knowledge assistant · multi-language interface · tenant configuration · AI cost guardrails · backup and restore.

## Customers and results
GARRISYNC is a commercial product with customer alliances running on it. This portfolio deliberately does not publish customer names, pricing agreements, revenue, or user counts.

## Takeaways
Product strategy under uncertainty, disciplined scope control, honest positioning, and shipping across the full stack — with AI tools as accelerators and human review as the gate.
