# My Role: Founder & AI Product / Operations Lead

- **Product strategy** — defined the alliance-operating-system positioning, feature priorities, and what *not* to build (for example, not competing with general chat platforms).
- **Planning** — wrote scoped task contracts for each workstream, sequenced releases, and kept a canonical project state record.
- **Implementation & delivery** — directed AI coding agents to build the Discord bot and web Command Center, then reviewed outputs against acceptance criteria before release.
- **Quality & release management** — acceptance reviews, staged deployments, rollback planning, and post-release verification.
- **Operations & infrastructure oversight** — hosting, backups, recovery rehearsals, and security hardening decisions.
- **Customer management** — onboarding alliances, collecting feedback, and turning it into prioritized improvements.
- **Business** — go-to-market planning, launch of a commercial SaaS product with paying customer alliances, customer relationships, and positioning/claims governance.
